Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026. Applies to the Riah TV website and the Riah TV app for Fire TV.

Short version: Riah TV counts views anonymously and nothing more. No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no cookies, and nothing that identifies you. (YouTube's player, used for Captain Mullet, may set its own cookies; see Google's policy below.)

What we collect

Only anonymous view counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics so we know how many people watch, roughly where in the world (country only), on what kind of device, and how fast the page loads. It sets no cookies, doesn't follow you to other sites, and doesn't keep your IP address. We can't tell who you are from it, and neither can anyone we'd show it to (Cloudflare's privacy policy).

To keep robots out of the people count, the page sorts each visit into one of three tallies: someone in a browser, the Fire TV app, or an automated visitor (a search crawler or AI agent). It decides from the browser's own name and whether anyone moves the mouse, touches the screen or presses a key in the first 30 seconds. It doesn't record what you did, only which tally to add one to.

The channel works out what's on from your device's clock and a schedule file. We don't ask for your name, email, location or anything else.

Who else you talk to while watching

The films don't live on our servers. While you watch, your device fetches them straight from where they're kept, and those services see the request the way any website would (your IP address and device type):

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this page and the schedule (GitHub's privacy statement)
  • The Internet Archive, where most films live (Internet Archive terms and privacy)
  • Wikimedia Commons, for some films and music (Wikimedia privacy policy)
  • YouTube, for Captain Mullet at 8 PM Mountain (Google privacy policy)

We don't get any of that information, and we don't sell or share anything with anyone.

Kids

Riah TV is made for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Changes and questions

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top first. Questions: hello@riahstudio.com.
