# Riah TV

A free 24/7 channel of classic comedy and cool stuff: the Marx Brothers,
Chaplin, Keaton, Harold Lloyd, Laurel & Hardy, early cartoons, Méliès, silent
horror, Blender's open movies and NASA footage. Captain Mullet airs every night
at 8:00 PM Mountain.

It's a plain static page. Your browser reads the clock and `lineup.json`, works
out what's on, and plays it straight from where each film lives (the Internet
Archive and Wikimedia Commons, plus YouTube for Captain Mullet), so everyone
watching sees the same thing at the same minute. Video starts muted.
